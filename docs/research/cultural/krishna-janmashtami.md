## 4. Distinctions requiring care

- **Story versus festival:** the birth story is the Bhagavata Purana's (Vaishnava); the guide attributes it so and does not tell Krishna's later life.
- **Smarta, Vaishnava, Gaudiya, Pushtimarg, ISKCON:** only Smarta/Vaishnava (news) and Gaudiya/ISKCON (HAF, ISKCON News) have any source; Pushtimarg has none. The guide never presents one reckoning as universal.
- **Midnight:** the text says "dark of night"; later tradition and several publishers say midnight.
- **Dahi Handi** is regional (western India), not pan-Hindu, and its legal rules are unsettled.
