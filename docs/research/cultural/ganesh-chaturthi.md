## 4. Distinctions requiring care

- **Birth stories are plural.** Never present one as the story; the guide cites "many tellings".
- **Public festival history is contested** in detail (Shivaji's role, 1892 versus 1893, Rangari versus Tilak). Attributed and hedged.
- **Tilak's political use of the festival** is historical context, kept to attributed, non-graphic statements; the Britannica "more communal" judgment is an owner decision.
- **Immersion rules are Indian.** US readers need "check local rules"; no US rule was found.
- **No fasting claim** is made; no source describes Ganesh Chaturthi fasting.
