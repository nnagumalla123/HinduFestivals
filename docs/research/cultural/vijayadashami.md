## 4. Distinctions requiring care

- **Two stories, two victories.** The Rama and Durga traditions are kept separate and neither is "the" meaning of the day.
- **Dussehra, Vijayadashami and Dasara are not identical terms**, though often used for the same day; the guide's names section explains this.
- **Consistency:** Navaratri says it ends with Vijayadashami on the tenth day and tells the Durga story; Diwali says "Dussehra ... celebrates the victory over Ravana. Diwali is the homecoming." The draft repeats neither beyond a cross-reference.
- **Effigy burning** is a northern and western custom, not universal (Kullu and Mysuru do not burn Ravana).
- **Ravana as a revered figure** in some communities is not described (one vague line, no region).
